01

Introduction

Welcome to HesapPro ("Application", "App", "we", "us", or "our"). HesapPro is a small business accounting and management application for Android that helps you track sales, purchases, cash registers, current accounts (cari), and inventory.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By installing or using HesapPro, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

✅
Core principle: All your business data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your business data with any third party. Cloud backup is entirely optional and controlled by you.
02

03

How We Use Your Information

All data processing occurs on your device. We use the data you enter solely for the following purposes:

  • Displaying your business dashboard (net equity, cash totals, receivables/payables)
  • Generating PDF reports, receipts, and current account statements
  • Managing inventory levels and stock alerts
  • Barcode scanning (camera) to look up or add products
  • Synchronising your data across multiple devices via Firebase (only if you choose to enable cloud backup)
  • Managing your trial period and subscription status via Google Play Billing

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the application features you actively use.

04

App Permissions

HesapPro requests the following Android permissions:

  • 📷
    CAMERA Used exclusively to scan product barcodes. No photos are taken or stored by the app.
  • 👥
    READ_CONTACTS Used only when you tap "Import from Contacts" to auto-fill a new business contact's name and phone number. The app never reads your contacts in the background.

    INTERNET Required for optional Firebase cloud backup and Google Play Billing (subscription management).
  • 🖼️
    READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Used to save PDF reports to your device's storage so you can share or archive them. No personal photos are accessed.
⚠️
All permissions are opt-in at runtime. Denying a permission simply disables the corresponding feature; it does not affect core accounting functionality.
05

Cloud Backup & Firebase

HesapPro includes an optional cloud backup feature powered by Google Firebase Realtime Database. This feature is disabled by default.

If you choose to enable cloud backup:

  • You create an account with your email address and a password of your choice.
  • Your business data (contacts, transactions, inventory) is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and stored in Firebase under your unique user ID.
  • Data is bidirectionally synchronised across your devices to the same Firebase project.
  • You can delete your cloud data at any time by signing out and using the "Reset & Delete Configuration" option.

Firebase is a Google service. Their privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

✅
If you never enable cloud backup, no data ever leaves your device. The app is fully functional offline.
06

Subscriptions & Billing

HesapPro offers a 7-day free trial followed by optional paid subscription plans (monthly/yearly). Payments are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details.

Subscription status (active / inactive) is stored locally on your device. Google Play provides us with anonymised confirmation of whether a valid subscription exists; it does not share your personal payment information with us.

To cancel a subscription, visit Google Play Store → Subscriptions.

08

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or manually delete records. Uninstalling HesapPro removes the local database and all SharedPreferences.

If you used cloud backup, your data in Firebase is associated with your account UID. To delete it, sign in to the cloud section within the app and use "Reset & Delete Configuration", or contact us at the address below to request complete deletion.

09

Children's Privacy

HesapPro is designed for business use and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10

Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information:

  • Local database uses Android's built-in app sandbox (data is not accessible to other apps).
  • Cloud data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Firebase security rules restrict access to your data by your unique user ID only.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your cloud backup account.
